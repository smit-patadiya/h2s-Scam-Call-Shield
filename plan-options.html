🔄 Architecture & Real-Time Data Pipeline

How audio travels from the phone call through chunked evaluation to spoken Gujarati/Hindi intervention.

1
Audio Slicing
Browser or mic audio is sliced into 4-second buffers and resampled to 16 kHz mono WAV via Web Audio API.
OfflineAudioContext • 16kHz
2
Cumulative Context
Chunk base64 is dispatched to /api/analyze along with transcript history so risk score builds cumulatively.
POST /api/analyze {audio, context}
3
Gemini Reasoning
Gemini 4 Argon checks for 8 red flags (urgency, threats, digital arrest, OTPs) with structured JSON schema output.
verdict: safe | suspicious | scam
4
Spoken Intervention
If scam detected, server generates reassuring Gujarati/Hindi speech via Gemini TTS and interrupts immediately.
speak(warning, 'Kore') • TTS
5
Guardian Dispatch
Pre-fills WhatsApp emergency alert for children: "Your father may be on a scam call right now. Please call them."
wa.me/{phone}?text={alert}
